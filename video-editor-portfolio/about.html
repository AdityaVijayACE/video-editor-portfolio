I'm Aditya Vijay, a video editor focused on storytelling, pacing, and visual impact.

I started editing in 2022 through AMV videos on YouTube. What began as a hobby became something I genuinely wanted to master. Today, I focus on video editing for creators and brands, with a particular interest in motion graphics, AI-assisted editing, and short-form storytelling.

My Story

My editing journey started in 2022 from a hospital room, where I began making AMV edits simply because I enjoyed the process. I knew the channel would never be easy to monetize because of the copyrighted music I used, but that was never enough reason to stop. I kept learning, experimenting, and pushing myself to make each edit better.

Over time, editing moved from a hobby into professional work. I worked with agencies including Apex Vision and Flux Vision, editing short-form content such as celebrity podcast clips while continuing to build my own portfolio through personal projects. One of the projects I'm most proud of is my COCA-COLA AI AD, which challenged me creatively and technically and pushed me to explore how AI can become part of the editing process.

Today, I'm focused on becoming a stronger editor and a better creative problem-solver. I don't see editing as simply putting clips together. A good editor helps solve a communication problem — whether the goal is building a personal brand, increasing attention, explaining an idea, or creating an emotional connection with the audience.

My Approach

I start by understanding what the video is trying to achieve. I watch the raw footage multiple times, study the script, and identify where visuals, B-roll, text, motion, or other supporting elements can strengthen the story. From there, I research visual references across platforms such as YouTube, Pinterest, and social media before building the edit.

My priorities are emotion, storytelling, pacing, and visual clarity. Technical execution matters, but technique is only valuable when it improves the message. I also believe an editor should be willing to challenge an idea when something can be improved. My goal isn't to blindly execute instructions — it's to contribute to the project and help make the final result stronger.
