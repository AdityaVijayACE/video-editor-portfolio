About This Project

COCA-COLA AI AD is a self-directed commercial concept built around cinematic storytelling and AI-assisted visual creation. The goal was to create a short-form advertisement that feels polished, energetic, and brand-focused while maintaining a clear visual narrative from beginning to end.

I handled the creative direction and edit, combining AI-generated visuals with commercial-style pacing, transitions, motion design, sound design, and finishing. The edit was built around rhythm, visual continuity, and controlled product-focused moments rather than simply presenting a sequence of AI shots.

What I Did

  • Creative direction and visual storytelling
  • AI-assisted visual development and shot selection
  • Cinematic editing, transitions, and pacing
  • Sound design, finishing, and commercial-style presentation

Key Highlights

  • Sound design and finishing designed to strengthen the commercial impact
